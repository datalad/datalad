### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Files in a tarball containing symlinks or hard links can again be
  retrieved from the `datalad-archives` special remote on filesystems
  without links, such as VFAT, with 7-Zip 21 or later: the links are
  extracted as copies of their targets.
  ([PR #7994](https://github.com/datalad/datalad/pull/7994))
  (by [@yarikoptic](https://github.com/yarikoptic))
