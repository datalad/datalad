### 🐛 Bug Fixes

- Close a subprocess's stdout/stderr pipes as soon as the reader thread sees
  EOF, so a consumer that stops reading early (e.g. `GitRepo.is_with_annex()`)
  no longer leaks file descriptors until garbage collection.  Fixes
  [#7932](https://github.com/datalad/datalad/issues/7932) via
  [PR #7971](https://github.com/datalad/datalad/pull/7971)
  (by [@syahra712](https://github.com/syahra712))
