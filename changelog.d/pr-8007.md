### 🚀 Enhancements and New Features

- `run` and `rerun` accept `-R/--recursion-limit LEVELS` to limit how many
  levels of subdatasets get saved after the command, e.g. `-R 0` to leave
  changes in input subdatasets uncommitted.
  ([PR #8007](https://github.com/datalad/datalad/pull/8007))
  (by [@asmacdo](https://github.com/asmacdo))
