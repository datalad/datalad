### 🚀 Enhancements and New Features

- Shell completion now offers sibling names (e.g. `push --to`, `update -s`),
  branches and tags (e.g. `diff --from`), procedures (`create -c`,
  `run-procedure`), only directories for `-d`/`-C`, and choices of more
  options (e.g. `--reckless`, `-f`).
  ([PR #7984](https://github.com/datalad/datalad/pull/7984))
  (by [@yarikoptic](https://github.com/yarikoptic))
